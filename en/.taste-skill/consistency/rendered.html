Tools for the stage. From import to foot switch.

Imports songs from Word

Paste text from Word, a notepad or ChordPro. Titles and chords are recognised, many songs at once.

Transposes with a tap

Shift the key by half steps. Capo and tempo stay saved with each song.

Listens to your foot switch

Bluetooth pedal or keyboard: give every key a single, double and long press.

Keeps your gigs apart

Separate repertoires for weddings, restaurants or concerts. One song can sit in several, edits apply everywhere.

Questions

What musicians ask before they start.

Email a question

Do I need an account?

No. StagePal works without signing up. Purchases go through your Google Play account.

Where are my songs stored?

Only on your device. A backup saves everything to a file, so you can move to a new device.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. After installing, everything runs offline, rhythm backing included.

Are song lyrics included?

No. You add your own songs or import them from Word, text files and ChordPro.

Which foot switches work?

Any Bluetooth pedal that works like a keyboard, such as page-turner pedals.

Is it coming to iPhone and iPad?

Yes. The iOS version follows after the Android launch.

Test StagePal before launch

For the test before the Google Play launch we are looking for musicians with an Android device. Write us a line about the device you play on.
